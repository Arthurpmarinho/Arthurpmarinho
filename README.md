## What I have worked on

- **BMS for an electric bus**, UnB research with Mercedes-Benz. Voltage and current acquisition circuit with LTC6811 and INA260, validated on an 8-cell Li-ion pack, data processed in MATLAB and Simulink.
- **Constant current regulator firmware**, internship at LEDS. C on ESP32 with ESP-IDF and FreeRTOS, RS-485 Modbus RTU link, validated on the bench.
- **Capital Rocket Team**. Four PCBs for a hybrid rocket and its ground station. The team won the hybrid rocket category at LASC 2025.
- **Micromouse**. Led the electronics, two PCBs in KiCad, first place in the UnB internal competition.
- **Motor vibration monitoring**, UnB with Eldorado and Rossi. Python acquisition software over Modbus RTU with FFT and ISO 10816 diagnostics, deployed on 20 motors.
- **SLAM with LiDAR** at ENSEA, in progress.
